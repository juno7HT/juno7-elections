# Donor Roadmap And Budget

## Budget Status

The budget below is an indicative planning hypothesis close to USD 400,000 over
12 months. It is not a final budget, procurement result, contract value or
official funding request.

All amounts should be reviewed against scope, local operating costs, security
requirements, procurement rules and the final election calendar.

## Indicative 12-Month Budget

| Category | Hypothesis |
| --- | ---: |
| Product and engineering | USD 135,000 |
| Database, infrastructure readiness and DevOps | USD 55,000 |
| Security, audit and governance support | USD 55,000 |
| Field operations design and training | USD 65,000 |
| Data quality, QA and test operations | USD 45,000 |
| Documentation, donor reporting and stakeholder workshops | USD 25,000 |
| Contingency and reserve | USD 20,000 |
| Total indicative envelope | USD 400,000 |

## Quarterly Roadmap

### Quarter 1 - Governance And Pilot Design

Expected deliverables:

- approved V2 operating assumptions;
- official review of electoral offices and constituency definitions;
- legal review of vote category definitions;
- pilot implementation plan;
- security and data protection review plan;
- training curriculum outline;
- refined budget and procurement assumptions.

### Quarter 2 - Controlled Pilot And Training

Expected deliverables:

- controlled pilot environment;
- field and data entry simulation;
- PV reception, entry, validation and correction exercises;
- dashboard demonstration with approved fictitious or pilot data;
- incident and rollback tabletop exercise;
- first donor progress report.

### Quarter 3 - Production Readiness

Expected deliverables:

- hardened deployment plan;
- monitoring and audit reporting procedures;
- role and permission matrix;
- correction approval workflow;
- publication governance process;
- performance and resilience test results;
- independent security review findings and remediation plan.

### Quarter 4 - Operational Readiness And Handover

Expected deliverables:

- final readiness assessment;
- operations handbook;
- training completion evidence;
- donor evidence pack;
- audit review package;
- go or no-go decision record;
- sustainability and maintenance plan.

## Milestone Gates

Each quarter should close with a documented decision gate:

- scope confirmed;
- risks reviewed;
- budget variance explained;
- security issues tracked;
- governance decisions updated;
- next-quarter work authorized.

## Assumptions

The budget assumes:

- reuse of the current Juno7 Elections codebase;
- no large hardware procurement included;
- local or approved managed infrastructure selected later;
- moderate field training scope;
- no production activation before governance and security approval;
- donor reporting in English and French if required.

## Cost Drivers

The final budget may increase or decrease based on:

- number of polling stations and data entry sites;
- number of training cohorts;
- hosting and disaster recovery requirements;
- legal and audit review depth;
- integration with document storage or identity systems;
- election calendar compression.

## Funding Recommendation

Fund the next phase as a controlled 12-month readiness program, not as a one-time
software build. The most important funded outcomes are governance clarity,
operational training, auditability, security assurance and a reliable publication
process.
